# PolyAI
PolyAI builds voice AI agents that answer customer calls for businesses. Dialog-RSN-1 is PolyAI's own voice model, and it's what Raven runs on: it listens to the caller's audio directly, decides when they've finished talking, and replies.

For anything about PolyAI beyond this, such as prices, policies or accounts, point people to poly.ai rather than guessing.
